Why most traders stay stuck

The market is not your biggest problem.

Guessing is.

  • Guessing when to enter.
  • Guessing how much to risk.
  • Guessing whether you should hold.
  • Guessing whether you should get out.
  • Taking a trade because something looked good.
  • Watching five different markets because somebody online said they were moving.
  • Adding another indicator every time the last strategy does not work.
And when real money is involved? Guessing gets expensive.

Successful trading is not about predicting every move the market will make.

It is about having rules for what you will do when the market moves.

That is what DeMark is teaching on August 30.

ENTRYWhat has to be on the screen before you are allowed to click.
RISKThe number you decide to lose before you are in the trade.
HOLDWhat keeps you in a trade that is working.
EXITWhat tells you it is time to get out, win or lose.

Four decisions. Most traders only have a plan for the first one.
