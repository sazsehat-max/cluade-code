01

Mindset

How to stop trading from emotion

Before we talk charts, we are talking about the trader behind the chart.

Fear. Greed. Impatience. Revenge trading. Overconfidence. The need to make your money back on the very next trade.

The shift is being willing to be wrong quickly instead of needing to be right.

The market

How DeMark trades the Dow

US30 and YM. One market, watched closely, instead of ten markets watched badly.

  • Why he stopped jumping between instruments
  • What he is reading across multiple timeframes
  • What a normal session actually looks like on his screen
03

Setups

How to identify high probability setups

Not stock picks. Not signals. A repeatable checklist of what has to be on the screen before he is allowed to click.

And just as important, what makes him sit on his hands and skip the trade.

04

Exits

The exit rules that tell you when to get out

Most accounts are not killed by bad entries. They are killed by holding losers too long and cutting winners too early.

Every exit rule is written before the entry, not during it.

05

Risk

Risk and wealth management

How much of the account goes into one trade, how position size is decided, and what happens after a losing day.

Trading is the skill that can create cash flow. What you do with that cash flow afterwards is what builds something lasting.

06

Getting started

How someone can begin learning with $300

DeMark recommends $300 to $500 to start. He will walk through what that account is realistically for.

A small account is for learning the process. It is not for replacing income.

He is also breaking down the thinking behind the session where he made $45,000 trading the Dow. The money is not the lesson. The decisions before the result are.
